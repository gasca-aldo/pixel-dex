# Pixel Dex Brand Workbook

Living brand document · Updated September 29, 2026.

This describes the brand direction. It does not claim that every future journaling or ownership capability is already implemented. Preserve existing records; do not imply that removing an item preserves its notes automatically.

## Brand category

Personal gaming journal

## Tagline

Your games. Your hardware. Your journey.

## Primary CTA

Start your Pixel Dex

## One-line definition

Pixel Dex is a personal gaming journal for your games, hardware, and journey.

## Purpose

Help people preserve and express their personal history with video games.

## Functional promise

One place to keep games, hardware, progress, backlog, upcoming releases, notes, reviews and personal gaming history.

## Emotional promise

A beautiful way to stay connected with the games, hardware and experiences that shaped your gaming life.

## Audience

Gamers who play across platforms and want a lasting record of what they have experienced.

## Core object

The gaming experience, not merely the owned item.

## Ownership philosophy

Ownership is useful context, but selling, trading or losing access to a game does not erase the experience.

## Journal philosophy

Notes can range from a quick thought to something closer to a short article.

## Backlog philosophy

No backlog guilt.
Playing, completed, paused, backlog and dropped are all legitimate parts of someone's gaming history.

## Hardware philosophy

Hardware is the physical connection to games and can carry its own memories and emotional value.

## Upcoming philosophy

Anticipation, hype and expectations before release are part of the gaming experience too.

## Social philosophy

Pixel Dex supports expression, notes, small reviews and sharing.
It is not intended to replace communities such as Reddit or become an engagement-driven social network.

## Monetary-value philosophy

Pixel Dex focuses on the experience and art behind gaming rather than collection pricing or speculation.

## Product personality

- Clean
- Simple
- Friendly
- Fresh
- Modern

## Voice

Human, clear, calm and enthusiastic about games.
Avoid:

- corporate marketing language
- forced gaming slang
- speculation/investment terminology
- backlog guilt
- artificial urgency
- exaggerated claims

## Product feeling

A personal archive you enjoy looking through.

## Sharing promise

A shared Pixel Dex should communicate:
This is who I am as a gamer and what I enjoyed most.

## Internal brand principle

A game doesn’t stop being part of your story when you finish it, drop it, or stop owning it.

## Beta philosophy

Pixel Dex is being built with real players. Beta feedback should shape usability, journaling, collection management and the overall experience.

## Product boundaries

Pixel Dex is not primarily:

- a price tracker
- an investment/speculation tool
- a storefront
- a social network
- a news site
- a completion-pressure system

## Landing-page messaging hierarchy

### 5 seconds

Pixel Dex is your personal gaming journal.

### 20 seconds

Track games, hardware and your journey across playing, backlog, completed, dropped and upcoming experiences.

### 1 minute

Understand that Pixel Dex preserves the story around gaming: ownership, memories, notes, hardware, anticipation and personal identity.
